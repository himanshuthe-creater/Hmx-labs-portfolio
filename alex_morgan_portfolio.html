02 / A little about me

Good work.
Good people.

I’m Alex — a curious creative who believes the best digital work is useful, considered, and full of feeling.

I partner with ambitious people and small teams to make brands and digital products feel unmistakably themselves. My process blends sharp thinking with playful exploration, and I care as much about the details under the hood as the ones on the screen.

When I’m not at my desk, you’ll find me in a bookshop, out walking with a camera, or collecting references for a project that doesn’t exist yet.
